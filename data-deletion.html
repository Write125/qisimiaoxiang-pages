User Data Deletion Instructions

Bayme operates internal advertising tools that connect to Meta (Facebook) Marketing API and TikTok Marketing API for accounts we own or are authorized to manage. This page explains how to request deletion of personal data we control in connection with those tools.

1. Who may request deletion

  • Authorized employees or contractors whose data we store for tool access
  • Business representatives of advertiser accounts we manage, where applicable
  • End users who believe we hold their personal data through our apps (uncommon for our internal tools)

2. What we can delete

Upon a verified request, we may delete or anonymize, where technically feasible:

  • OAuth access tokens and refresh tokens stored for API access
  • Internal logs tied to your identity (email, user ID, advertiser ID references)
  • Exported reports or configuration backups that contain personal identifiers you provided

Data held only on Meta or TikTok platforms (e.g. ad accounts, creatives, insights) must also be requested from those platforms where applicable. We will revoke our stored tokens and stop further API access for your account upon confirmed deletion.

3. How to submit a request

Email dev@bayme.store with the subject line Data Deletion Request and include:

  • Your full name and contact email
  • Platform (Meta / TikTok / both)
  • Relevant account IDs (e.g. Facebook user ID, ad account ID, TikTok advertiser ID) if known
  • A description of what you want deleted

4. Verification and timing

  • We may ask for reasonable proof of identity or authorization before processing.
  • We aim to acknowledge requests within 5 business days and complete deletion within 30 days, unless law requires retention.
  • Some data may be retained where required for legal, security, or fraud-prevention obligations.
