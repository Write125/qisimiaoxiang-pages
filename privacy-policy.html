Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Bayme (“we”) handles information when authorized staff use our internal tools that connect to TikTok Marketing API and related TikTok for Developers products. This site (www.bayme.store) hosts our company information, legal policies, and OAuth callbacks. It does not offer public user registration.

TikTok Marketing API: We use the TikTok Marketing API only for internal advertising analytics and operations on advertiser accounts we own or are explicitly authorized to manage. We do not collect, purchase, or resell TikTok end-user personal data through this website, and we do not operate a consumer data broker service.

1. Who this applies to

This policy applies to authorized employees and contractors who use Bayme’s internal advertising tools. It does not describe a consumer-facing social application open to the general public.

2. Information we collect and use

Through TikTok OAuth and Marketing API, we may process:

  • OAuth tokens (access token, refresh token) to call the API on behalf of authorized advertisers
  • Advertiser and account identifiers (e.g. advertiser_id, business center IDs)
  • Advertising data — campaign, ad group, and ad names/IDs; spend, impressions, clicks, conversions, and related metrics returned by the API
  • Creative metadata — video/image IDs, ad copy fields, and statuses needed to create or update ads
  • Technical logs — API request errors, timestamps, and internal job logs for troubleshooting (not published)

We use this information solely to operate, report on, and optimize TikTok advertising for accounts Bayme owns or is authorized to manage.

3. Website visitors

This public website is informational. We do not use third-party advertising cookies on these pages or sell visitor analytics to data brokers. Standard web server logs may record IP address, user agent, and requested URL for security and uptime.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal data from TikTok end-users through this site.
  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not provide Marketing API access to unrelated third parties.

5. Legal bases (where applicable)

We process data based on legitimate business interests in running our advertising operations, performing contracts with platforms, and complying with law. Authorized users consent to TikTok’s authorization screens when connecting accounts.

6. Storage and security

  • Tokens and API secrets are stored on private servers or secure configuration — not embedded in this static website.
  • Access is restricted to personnel who need it for their job duties.
  • We use HTTPS for all public pages and OAuth redirects.

7. Sharing

We may share data only with TikTok as required to use their APIs, with infrastructure providers under confidentiality obligations, or when required by law.

8. Retention

We retain tokens and advertising data only as long as needed for operations, security, and legal compliance, then delete or anonymize where reasonable.

9. International transfers

Data may be processed in countries where our servers or TikTok operate. We take reasonable steps to protect data consistent with this policy.

10. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding personal data we hold about you as an employee/contractor. Contact dev@bayme.store. TikTok end-user requests should be directed to TikTok where applicable.

11. Children

Our internal tools are not directed at children under 16.

12. Changes

We may update this policy; the “Last updated” date will change.

13. Contact

Privacy questions: dev@bayme.store · Contact page
