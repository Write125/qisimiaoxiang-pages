About Bayme

Bayme operates as a direct merchant and brand operator—not a public software marketplace. Our team handles product, supply chain, customer support, and paid media in-house.

📦

Product & catalog

We develop seasonal apparel lines (tees, essentials, limited graphics), maintain SKU data, and publish our catalog on www.bayme.store.

🎯

Growth & advertising

Media buyers run TikTok and other paid channels. We use TikTok for Business and apply for Marketing API access to automate reporting and campaign updates our team already performs manually.

🤝

Partners & accounts

Advertising may run on advertiser accounts we own or accounts assigned to our staff in partner Business Centers, with explicit TikTok authorization only.

Markets we serve

Our largest paid-media focus is Japan (Japanese creatives, JPY/USD blended ops). We also ship to North America and select EU destinations.

  • Japan: Short-form video, localized landing pages, 7–12 day standard shipping
  • North America: English catalog, USD checkout
  • Europe: Selected countries; duties may apply at import
