About Bayme

Bayme is a cross-border e-commerce and digital marketing team focused on building and scaling independent apparel brands for international shoppers—with TikTok as a core growth channel.

Who we are

We are operators, not a software marketplace. Our team handles product selection, creative production, media buying, customer support, and internal tooling. We sell through our own online catalog and invest in paid social—primarily TikTok—for customer acquisition in Japan and other regions.

Bayme registered a TikTok for Business profile as a China cross-border export merchant (出海). Our Business Center is named Bayme. We may advertise on advertiser accounts we create or on accounts assigned to our staff in partner Business Centers, always with explicit TikTok authorization.

What we believe

  • Clarity over hype: Straightforward product pages, honest shipping times, and readable policies.
  • Creative discipline: Test short-form ads systematically; scale winners, pause losers quickly.
  • Responsible API use: Marketing API access supports internal analytics and campaign operations—we do not resell API access.
